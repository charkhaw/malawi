# 6. Resources and Capacity Building

## 6.1 Resource and skill matrix

| Role | Change responsibilities and engagement |
|---|---|
| Training and Change Management Specialist | Owns this plan: impact on each group, support to NRB's engagement, procedure adoption, readiness evidence at each gate, and reinforcement after go-live. Also leads the training program, as in Preliminary Project Plan Section 3.8. Weeks 1 to 32, on site from week 15 |
| Supplier's Representative | Coordination with the Purchaser's Project Manager; Monthly Progress Report |
| Solution Architect | Impact analysis of technical changes; Supplier technical lead on the change board until handover |
| Software Integration Specialist | Software releases and interface changes |
| Secure Personalization System Engineer and Card Mailing System Engineer, with the equipment manufacturer | Equipment and firmware changes; content of the procedures for the production and mailing lines |
| Cybersecurity and PKI Specialist | Security impact of changes; incident manager for emergency changes until handover |
| NRB process owners, ICT manager and trainers | Counterparts who take over each process, the change-control roles and the training of new staff |

The Training and Change Management Specialist holds a degree in ICT, education or human resource
development and has at least five years delivering ICT training to government staff, operators and
technical administrators. The CV and certificates are provided with the Key Personnel forms.

## 6.2 Capacity building and knowledge transfer

The staged transfer of responsibility to NRB, from factory training to independent operation, is the
knowledge transfer plan of Preliminary Project Plan Section 3.1 and is not repeated here. The change
side adds the transfers below.

| What NRB takes over | How | Transfer criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Process and procedure ownership | Procedures written with NRB staff, taught from in training, and corrected in pilot production and coaching | Procedures proven usable in operational acceptance test 16 |
| Change control | NRB administrators and approvers run changes under observation in weeks 27 to 30 | NRB has run a normal change and a rehearsed emergency change end to end, with the Supplier observing |
| Training of new staff | Six NRB trainers are certified after delivering the cover group under observation in weeks 27 and 28 | Trainer certificates |
| Security operations | As set out in Cybersecurity Risk Management Plan Section 9.4 | The transfer criteria there |

**Reinforcement.** The change is sustained after go-live. Resident engineers review production with NRB
every day during stabilization in weeks 31 and 32. Adoption issues are raised in the monthly service
review. NRB's trainers train new staff from the same materials. Users are briefed on each new release
before it is applied under change control.
