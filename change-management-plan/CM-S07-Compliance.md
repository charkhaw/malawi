# 7. Compliance

## 7.1 Compliance statement

The Proposer confirms that, if awarded the Contract, it will:

- provide the Training and Change Management Specialist required by Section VII clause 2.4.1.11 throughout supply and installation;
- deliver the transition in the sequence and through the readiness gates of Section 4, without moving a contractual milestone;
- operate the change management framework of Section VII clause 1.4.1.2 for all components through the warranty period, as set out in Section 5 and System Architecture Section 12.1;
- support NRB's stakeholder engagement under GCC 9.13;
- route every change to scope, price or time through GCC 39, and keep the Change Order Log;
- agree the requirements freeze date under GCC 39.1.5 in the Project Plan;
- report change and readiness status monthly, as set out in Section 3.1.
