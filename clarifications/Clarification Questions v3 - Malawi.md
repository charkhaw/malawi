**1\. Scoring scale (Section III, pages 57 and 64\)**

Page 57 states that each sub-feature is scored on a 4/2/0 scale, in which 4 denotes *"Fully Compliant – feature present and meets all requirements with clear evidence"*.

Page 64 states that the evaluation committee will assign *"a whole number score from 0 to 4"*, in which the scoring is *"1 for the feature being present but showing deficiencies; 2 for meeting the requirements; 3 for marginally exceeding the requirements; and 4 for significantly exceeding the requirements"*.

A fully compliant response therefore scores 4 under page 57 but 2 under page 64\.

*Please confirm which scale governs the evaluation of the Technical Part.*

**2\. NRIS technical documentation (Section VII §2.1)**

Section VII §2.1.3.1 requires the Supplier to design, develop, test and deploy all interfaces between NRIS and the Personalization System, and §2.1.3.2 requires the Supplier to analyse NRIS data structures and map NRIS data fields to the personalization system.

*Please confirm what NRIS technical documentation will be made available to the successful Supplier (in particular existing interface control documents, API specifications, data dictionary, database platform and network topology) and at what stage of the implementation schedule it will be provided.*

**3- Pre-Printed Card Body Backgrounds vs. On-Site Personalization** 

Please confirm that the 2,000,000 polycarbonate cards are to be delivered to the Central Printing Facility as pre-printed card bodies incorporating all static security artwork, Guilloche backgrounds, UV printing, and the Optically Variable Magnetic Ink (OVMI) Malawi flag motif, such that local on-site card personalization is limited to the laser engraving of individual citizen biographical and biometric data 

**4- Resolution of Full-Color vs. Grayscale Portrait Personalization**

Section VII §3.2.2.1.1 and Section III §1.4 state that the card portrait must be personalized "in full colour" or require "Stacked-Layer Full-Color personalization. Conversely, Section VII §3.1.11 and §3.4.4 explicitly mandate "greyscale printing technology" and "Stacked-Layer Grey Scale Personalization" on the cards.

Please resolve this technical contradiction and confirm if the personalized citizen portrait is to be laser-engraved in high-resolution grayscale (monochrome) on-site using the personalization systems supplied under this contract, and if the "full colour" requirement in §3.2.2.1.1 refers only to the static background artwork pre-printed by the card OEM

**5- Clarification of Card Body Chip / Smart Card Requirements**

Please clarify whether the 2,000,000 polycarbonate cards to be supplied under this contract must incorporate an electronic chip. If a chip is required, please provide the mandatory chip type (Contactless, Contact, or Dual-Interface) and the memory/cryptographic requirements

**6\. Commencement of the Warranty Period**

SCC GCC 29.4 provides that the Warranty Period *"shall begin from the date of Final Acceptance of the System and extend for 3 Years"*. SCC GCC 7.3 requires the supply of spare parts *"for three (3) years beginning with Operational Acceptance"*, and Section VII §5.1.1.1 specifies a three-year Warranty Defect Repair coverage period.

*Please confirm whether the Warranty Period commences upon Operational Acceptance, and if "Final Acceptance" is intended as a distinct milestone, please clarify its definition and the event that establishes it.*

**8\. Envelope and carrier letter specifications**

§3.3 requires card affixing to carrier letters at one to four cards per letter

No envelope format or dimensions, paper size, paper weight or carrier letter specification is stated anywhere in the Requirements.

*Please provide the envelope format(s) and dimensions and the carrier letter paper size and weight to be accommodated, and confirm whether envelopes and carrier letter stock are to be supplied by the Purchaser or by the Supplier.*

**9\. Mailing system throughput requirement**

§3.3.10 requires "At least 2,000 completed mailers per hour with 02 machines", however, §3.3.1 permits one to four cards per carrier letter.

Where more than one card is affixed per letter, 2,000 mailers per hour and 2,000 cards per hour are not equivalent, and the two figures may differ by a factor of up to four.

*Please confirm whether the required throughput is 2,000 mail pieces (envelopes) per hour or 2,000 cards per hour.*

**10\. Sorting criterion**

§3.3 requires "Continuous sorting and dispatch sorting". The criterion by which mail pieces are to be sorted is not stated.

*Please confirm the sorting criterion required (for example by district, courier zone, postal route or dispatch batch) as this determines the number of sorting outputs the mailing equipment must provide.*

**11\. Language requirements**

§3.0.1 requires all delivered components to support the English language. §3.1.4 requires the personalization system to "Produce multilingual identity documents". §3.4.4 requires "QR Code, and multilingual data" as a personalization security feature. §1.3.1.1.2.1.2 requires the user interface to provide "Multilingual support".

*Please confirm which languages are required to appear on the identity card, and which languages the system user interface must support.*
