## A. Evaluation and scoring

**1. Scoring scale — Section III, pages 57 and 64**

Page 57 states that each sub-feature is scored on a 4/2/0 scale, in which 4 denotes *"Fully Compliant
– feature present and meets all requirements with clear evidence"*.

Page 64 states that the evaluation committee will assign *"a whole number score from 0 to 4"*, in
which the scoring is *"1 for the feature being present but showing deficiencies; 2 for meeting the
requirements; 3 for marginally exceeding the requirements; and 4 for significantly exceeding the
requirements"*.

A fully compliant response therefore scores 4 under page 57 but 2 under page 64.

*Please confirm which scale governs the evaluation of the Technical Part.*

**2. Scope of Category II — Section III, Table 2, page 60**

Category II is titled *"Integration to the National Registration and Identity System (NRIS)"* and
carries 30% of the technical score. However, the four sub-criteria listed under it, and their
guidance notes, address the laser personalization system: personalization laser feature support
(10%), marking resolution requirements (8%), independent card processing capability (8%), and duplex
card printing speed (4%).

*Please confirm whether Category II will be scored solely against those four listed sub-criteria, or
whether the NRIS integration requirements set out in Section VII §2.1 are also scored within this
category. If the latter, please indicate the applicable sub-criterion and weight.*

**3. Reference to a "Technical Team" category — Section III, page 57**

The scoring approach is stated to apply to *"All categories beside Technical Team"*. No Technical
Team category appears in Table 1 or Table 2, and the five categories listed there already total 100%.

*Please confirm that there is no separately scored Technical Team category.*

## B. Pricing and recurrent costs

**4. Treatment of recurrent costs in the evaluated price**

Section III §4 (pages 66–67) provides that recurrent costs will be evaluated on the basis of Price
Schedules 3.3 and 3.5 and that *"Such costs shall be added to the Proposal price for evaluation"*,
and sets out a net present value calculation.

PDS 36.1(f)(b) states *"Present value of Recurrent Costs: No"*, and PDS ITP 39.1 states that the
discount rate for net present value calculations of recurrent costs is *"Not Applicable"*.

*Please confirm whether recurrent costs are to be added to the evaluated Proposal price and, if so,
the discount rate to be applied.*

**5. Recurrent Cost Items and the System Inventory Table — Section VII, page 197**

The System Inventory Table (Recurrent Cost Items) lists Warranty Defect Repair and
Software/Firmware Licenses and Updates, and against each for Years 1 to 3 states *"All items, all
sites, included in the Supply and Install Price"*. PDS ITP 17.2 states *"The Proposer must propose
Recurrent Cost Items"*, and Price Schedule 3.3 requires recurrent costs to be carried to the Grand
Summary Cost Table.

*Please confirm whether Year 1 to Year 3 recurrent costs are to be entered as nil in Price Schedule
3.3 on the basis that they are included in the Supply and Installation price, or whether they are to
be priced separately in that Schedule.*

**6. Pricing of the Post-Warranty Services Period**

SCC GCC 1.1(e)(xiii) establishes a Post-Warranty Services Period of thirty-six (36) months
commencing on completion of the Warranty Period. Section VII §5.2.1.3 requires post-warranty
maintenance services for three (3) years, renewable. Price Schedule 3.5 is headed *"Warranty
Period"*, and the Recurrent Cost System Inventory Table addresses Years 1 to 3 only.

*Please confirm in which Price Schedule the thirty-six month Post-Warranty Services Period is to be
priced.*

**7. Separate contracts for Recurrent Cost items — Section IV, page 111**

Item (e) of the Format of the Technical Proposal requires *"any separate and enforceable contract(s)
for Recurrent Cost items which the PDS ITP 17.2 required Proposers to propose"*. PDS ITP 17.2 states
*"The Proposer must not propose for contracts for Recurrent Cost Items not included in the main
Contract"*.

*Please confirm that item (e) is not applicable to this procurement.*

## C. Site information, existing systems and card design

**8. Site drawings and site survey information — Section VII §0.6, page 204**

Section §0.6 is titled *"Site Drawings and Site Survey Information Relevant to the Information
System"* and provides the postal and contact details of the National Registration Bureau. The scope
of supply includes the renovation of the allocated room to the specification set out in Section VII
§3.5, against a fixed 32-week period for Operational Acceptance to which liquidated damages attach.

*In order that all Proposers may design and price the renovation works on a common basis, please
provide:*

- *(a) floor plans and dimensions of the room allocated to the Card Production Facility;*
- *(b) the existing electrical supply capacity and single-line diagram;*
- *(c) details of existing HVAC, structured cabling and physical security provisions; and*
- *(d) any structural, architectural or as-built drawings available.*

**9. Records of the site visit — ITP 7.4 as amended by Addendum No. 1**

Addendum No. 1 amended ITP 7.4 to provide for a site visit conducted by the Purchaser on 31 August
2026 at the National Registration Bureau Head Office.

*Please provide the record or minutes of that site visit, together with copies of any drawings,
presentations, measurements or other information furnished to attendees, so that all Proposers hold
the same site information. Please also confirm whether a further site visit may be accommodated
before the deadline for submission of Proposals.*

**10. NRIS technology architecture diagram — Section VII §0.4.10, page 203**

Section §0.4.10.1 states that the NRIS technology architecture comprises front-end systems
(registration workstations, mobile enrolment kits, biometric capture devices), core systems (NID,
identity management platform, biometric identification system, central population register),
supporting systems (card personalization systems, mailing and dispatch systems, reporting and
analytics, document management, verification and authentication platforms) and infrastructure
(primary data centre, disaster recovery facility, network infrastructure, cybersecurity
infrastructure, backup and recovery systems). Section §0.4.10.2 then states *"The technology
architecture is depicted by the diagram below"*.

The diagram appearing at that location is titled "NRB Key Information Systems" and depicts the four
civil registration systems — eBRS, eMRS, eDRS and the NID System — at a conceptual level. It does not
depict the technology architecture components listed in §0.4.10.1, and in particular does not show
the Card Production Facility, the card personalization or mailing systems, the infrastructure layer,
or any interfaces between them.

*Please provide the technology architecture diagram referred to in §0.4.10.2, showing the components
listed in §0.4.10.1 and the interfaces between the NRIS and the Card Production Facility.*

**11. NRIS technical documentation — Section VII §2.1**

Section VII §2.1.3.1 requires the Supplier to design, develop, test and deploy all interfaces between
NRIS and the Personalization System, and §2.1.3.2 requires the Supplier to analyse NRIS data
structures and map NRIS data fields to the personalization system.

*Please confirm what NRIS technical documentation will be made available to the successful Supplier —
in particular existing interface control documents, API specifications, data dictionary, database
platform and network topology — and at what stage of the implementation schedule it will be provided.*

**12. Card design, approval and quantity — Section VII §3.4 and Section III §1.4**

Section VII §3.4 requires the supply of *"pre-personalized polycarbonate cards"* in a quantity of two
million. Section III §1.4 requires the card supplier to provide five thousand specimen cards free of
charge *"as per the approved design"*, and Section VII §3.4.5 requires sample card images
demonstrating *"the exact layout, design and security features"*.

*Please confirm:*

- *(a) that the two million cards are blank card bodies incorporating the card-body security features
  specified in §3.4.2 and §3.4.3, to be personalized in Malawi on the laser personalization systems
  supplied under this Contract; and*
- *(b) whether an approved card design and artwork will be issued by the Purchaser, or whether
  Proposers are to propose a card design for the Purchaser's approval.*

## D. Qualification and contractual matters

**13. CIRA registration of the rehabilitation subcontractor — Section III §1.4, page 54**

The minimum criteria for the subcontractor for Rehabilitation of the Card Production Facility include
registration with the Construction Industry Regulatory Authority (CIRA) in Malawi.

*Please confirm (a) whether a specific CIRA registration category and class is required, and (b)
whether CIRA registration must be in place at the deadline for submission of Proposals or may be
obtained prior to signature of the Contract.*

**14. Specific Experience, "At least one member" column — Section III, Sub-Factor 1.4.2, page 74**

The "JV: At least one member" cell of Sub-Factor 1.4.2 contains the instruction *"[specify any key
requirement to be met by one member]"*.

*Please confirm the requirement applicable to that column, or confirm that no such requirement
applies.*

**15. Cross-reference in Sub-Factor 1.2.3 — Section III, page 71**

Sub-Factor 1.2.3 (Pending Litigation) refers to *"criteria established in 2.3.1 below"*. There is no
clause 2.3.1 in Section III.

*Please confirm that the intended reference is Sub-Factor 1.3.1, Historical Financial Performance.*

**16. Commencement of the Warranty Period**

SCC GCC 29.4 provides that the Warranty Period *"shall begin from the date of Final Acceptance of the
System and extend for 3 Years"*. SCC GCC 7.3 requires the supply of spare parts *"for three (3) years
beginning with Operational Acceptance"*, and Section VII §5.1.1.1 specifies a three-year Warranty
Defect Repair coverage period.

*Please confirm whether the Warranty Period commences upon Operational Acceptance, and if "Final
Acceptance" is intended as a distinct milestone, please clarify its definition and the event that
establishes it.*
