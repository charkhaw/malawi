# INTERNAL — Questions deliberately withheld from the clarification request

**Do not send this file to PPPC.** It records the questions I did *not* put in
`Clarification-Request-MW-PPPC-546386-GO-RFB.md`, and why.

Three ambiguities are real, but asking about each carries a risk that the answer tightens a
requirement we may currently satisfy under the looser reading. These are commercial judgements, not
technical ones, so they are your call. Decide once you have the card OEM's actual figures.

---

## 1. Card OEM production capacity — Section III §1.4 item 2, page 54

**The text:** the card supplier must *"Have within its own manufacturing sites a card production
capacity of **up to** fifty (50) million cards per year"*.

**The ambiguity:** read literally, "up to" is a ceiling — any OEM producing 50 million or fewer
cards per year satisfies it. Almost certainly a drafting error for "at least".

**Why asking could hurt:** a clarification would very likely come back restating it as a **minimum of
50 million cards per year**, which would exclude a mid-size card OEM that the text as written admits.

**Decision rule:**
- If our card OEM is comfortably **above** 50M/year — ask. The clarity helps us and may exclude
  weaker competitors.
- If our card OEM is **below** 50M/year — do not ask. Evidence capacity against the text as written
  and let the looser reading stand.

---

## 2. Card OEM national identity references — Section III §1.4 item 2, pages 54–55

**The text:** *"Have at least two (2) National Identity references for the supply of at least five (5)
million polycarbonate cards. In total five (5) million cards collectively (Letter of References or
Purchase Orders will be required)."*

**The ambiguity:** the first sentence reads as 5 million per reference; the second sentence says
5 million **collectively**. The second is the lenient reading and it is explicit in the document.

**Why asking could hurt:** a clarification could rule that each of the two references must
individually cover 5 million cards — doubling the bar. We already have an explicit lenient reading in
the text to rely on.

**Decision rule:**
- Only ask if our OEM clears 5 million on **each** reference individually.
- Otherwise stay silent and evidence against "in total five (5) million cards collectively", quoting
  that sentence in the qualification submission.

## 3. Factor 1.3 applicability columns — Section III, pages 72–73

**The ambiguity:** whether the US$10,000,000 average annual turnover and the US$600,000 financial
resources requirement fall on a JV combined or on each JV member individually. The columns did not
extract reliably and should be read visually from the PDF.

**Why asking could hurt:** the question is only meaningful for a joint venture. Inkript is bidding as
a **single prime with subcontractors**, not as a JV, so the "Single Entity" column governs and
Inkript must meet both thresholds itself. Raising the question signals that we may be contemplating a
JV structure and invites scrutiny of it.

**Recommendation: do not send**, unless the bid structure changes to a joint venture. If it does,
this becomes an important question and the maximum number of JV members is three (PDS ITP 4.1).

Two questions were in my earlier draft and have been **dropped as unnecessary**, not withheld

**RESOLVED — and it went back in, sharper.** The diagram *is* present on p.203; my inability to see
it was an extraction limitation, not a defect in the RFP. But once seen, it raised a better question
than the one I had drafted.

The diagram is titled **"NRB Key Information Systems"** and shows four triangles — **eMRS**
(marriage), **eDRS** (death) and **eBRS** (birth) arranged around a central **NID System** hub — under
the strapline **"One Citizen · One ID · For Life"**. It is a conceptual civil-registration diagram.

§0.4.10.1, immediately above it, describes something entirely different: front-end systems, core
systems, **supporting systems including card personalization and mailing and dispatch systems**, and
an infrastructure layer of data centre, DR facility, network and cybersecurity infrastructure.
§0.4.10.2 then says *"The technology architecture is depicted by the diagram below"* — but the
diagram depicts none of those components, shows no Card Production Facility, and shows no interfaces.

That mismatch is now **Question 10** in the clarification letter, requesting the architecture diagram
that §0.4.10.2 actually refers to. It is a stronger question than "please provide the missing
diagram", because it quotes what the text promises and states precisely what the diagram omits.
