# 1. Functional, Architectural and Performance Requirements

**How to read this checklist.** This is the item-by-item commentary on the Technical Requirements
required by ITP 16.2(c), in the Technical Responsiveness Checklist format of Section IV. Its sections
follow the numbering of Section VII. For each requirement it gives the Section VII clause and a short
description, the technical reasons supporting compliance, and cross-references to the supporting
information in the System Architecture, the Preliminary Project Plan, the Cybersecurity Risk
Management Plan, the Change Management and Configuration Governance Plan and the attachments.

| **1.6.1** Design, implement and maintain multi-layered security ensuring confidentiality, integrity, availability and traceability. | Complies. The layered architecture applies zero trust, least privilege, segmentation, defense in depth and traceability, protecting transport, message, identity and storage independently and attributing every action to an identity and a time.
