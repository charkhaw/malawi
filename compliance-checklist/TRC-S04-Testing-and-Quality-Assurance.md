# 4. Testing and Quality Assurance Requirements

## 4.1 Inspections

| Requirement | Technical reasons supporting compliance | Cross-references |
|---|---|---|
| **4.1.1** Arrange Malawi team visits for FAT and training on laser personalization and mailing systems at the factory | Complies. Factory acceptance testing is held in week 17 at the equipment manufacturer's production facility, on the actual card stock, covering the personalization and mailing systems and witnessed by the NRB delegation. The manufacturer trains the delegation's technical staff there on equipment operation, calibration, preventive maintenance and first-line repair. Travel and boarding are borne by the Government of Malawi. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.5, 3.4 and 4.3; Attachment: FAT hosting confirmation |
| **4.1.2** Provisions and methods for inspecting data center and ancillary equipment on delivery and unpacking | Complies. Inspection on delivery is a defined test level: as goods are unpacked and placed at the Card Production Facility, the Supplier and NRB jointly verify they arrived complete, undamaged and as ordered, in week 15 for ICT infrastructure and week 21 for equipment. Results go in a delivery inspection record, and equipment serial numbers are recorded. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.5, 2.10 and 4.1 |

## 4.2 Pre-commissioning tests

| Requirement | Technical reasons supporting compliance | Cross-references |
|---|---|---|
| **4.2.0** Supplier performs the listed tests, with Purchaser assistance, before Installation Certificate issue | Complies. Beyond the manufacturer's standard set-up and commissioning, the Supplier performs all sixteen Table A tests with the Purchaser's assistance in weeks 19 to 26, the ICT estate from week 19 and the equipment from week 24. Successful completion and the Purchaser's approval of their reports are the entry criteria for the Installation Certificate gate in week 26. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.5, 4.5 and 4.7 |
| **4.2.1** Prepare, execute and document a full Pre-Commissioning Test Program; retest failures at no cost | Complies. The Pre-Commissioning Test Program covers the sixteen Table A tests across hardware, software, infrastructure, security, integration, operational and performance requirements, and is submitted for NRB approval in week 17. The Purchaser approves each test report before operational acceptance in week 27. A failed test is corrected and repeated at no cost, with every test the correction could affect. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.10, 4.1, 4.5 and 4.7 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 1** Physical installation verification: placement, anchoring, cabling, racks, workstations and safety clearances | Complies. In week 24, after the manufacturer's engineers have installed the equipment, the Supplier verifies placement and anchoring of the equipment, racks and workstations, cable management and labeling, and safety clearances. Building-level items are verified with the facility works, and rack elevations are fixed in the approved Low-Level Design. Output is the Physical Installation Verification Report. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.5, 2.10 and 4.5 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 2** Electrical power tests: utility, UPS, distribution, load, transfer switch, earthing, voltage and frequency | Complies. Electrical power testing is performed under the facility works, which install the uninterruptible power supply, generator support with automatic transfer, power distribution and grounding, and deliver the electrical system documentation. The Information System relies on that result, and thereafter monitors power and UPS status with threshold alerts. Temperature, humidity and power readings then feed the System's monitoring and alerting, and environmental monitoring events feed security event management. | **4.2.2 Table A, Test 4** Network tests: LAN, WAN, redundancy, switching, throughput, latency, VLANs, DNS and DHCP | Complies. In week 19 the Supplier tests LAN and WAN connectivity, redundancy, switch function, throughput, latency, VLANs, DNS and DHCP, reported in the Network Acceptance Report. Each security zone is a separate VLAN routed only through the firewall pair, and wide area links run on the Government Wide Area Network that the Purchaser provisions. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.7 and 4.5; System Architecture Section 9.2 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 5** Cybersecurity tests: Active Directory, RBAC, password policy, firewall, anti-malware, endpoint, audit logging | Complies. In week 19 the Supplier verifies Active Directory integration, role-based access, password policy, firewall rules, anti-malware, endpoint protection, log generation and event monitoring, reported in the Security Readiness Report. The password policy follows NIST SP 800-63B, and endpoint detection and response runs on every server, virtual machine and workstation. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 6.12, 10.3 and 10.7 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 6** Personalization equipment tests: card feeding, laser engraving, printing alignment and quality, barcode and QR encoding | Complies. In week 24 the Supplier tests hopper loading and automatic feeding, image, text and variable data engraving, alignment, greyscale consistency (the laser is monochrome) and QR encoding, with engraving quality verified in line on every card. As amended by Addendum 2, chip encoding is removed: the chipless card carries a signed QR code and a machine-readable card identifier. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 3.2, 3.6 and 4.4; Attachment: Laser personalization system datasheet |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 7** Mailing system tests: envelope feeding, insertion, address printing, sorting and batch processing | Complies. In week 24 the Supplier tests envelope feeding and insertion, address printing, sorting and batch processing, reported in the Mailing System Functional Test Report. Each line affixes one to four cards per carrier, inserts and seals automatically, prints the address on the carrier behind the envelope window, and outputs mail pieces sorted by destination. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 5.2, 5.3 and 5.5; Attachment: Mailing system datasheet |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 8** Software tests: operating system, database, application, licenses, backup configuration and monitoring tools | Complies. In week 19 the Supplier verifies operating systems, database, application installation, licenses, backup configuration and monitoring tools, reported in the Software Readiness Report. Installation uses hardened, version-controlled baseline templates from a central configuration repository, and every software title is held with its license terms in the classified software inventory. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 6.14 and 12.1 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 9** NRIS integration tests: API and web service connectivity, data retrieval, status updates, error handling | Complies. In week 25 the Supplier tests API and web service connectivity, secure communication, retrieval of demographic data, photograph and signature, status updates, and invalid record, missing data and communication failure handling. Error conditions are induced rather than inspected, and the NRIS interface uses mutual certificate authentication over TLS 1.2 or higher. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 7.12, 7.16 and 10.5 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 10** Data validation tests: field mapping, transformation, character encoding, completeness, duplicate detection | Complies. In week 25 the Supplier verifies field mapping, transformation, character encoding, completeness and duplicate record detection, reported in the Data Integrity Verification Report. Mapping follows the Data Mapping and Transformation Document field by field, every record is validated before queuing, and a duplicate request is never queued twice. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 7.3 and 7.12 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 11** Card security feature tests: engraving, ghost image, microtext, guilloche, background, UV, machine-readable elements | Complies. In week 25 the Supplier verifies laser engraving quality, the secondary (ghost) image, micro text, guilloche, security background, UV features and machine-readable elements, reported in the Card Security Features Verification Report. The card body features are those listed for the approved design, and the signed QR code and card identifier are read and validated on every card. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 3.6, 4.2 and 4.4 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 12** Quality control tests: automated inspection, defect detection, OCR, barcode and image quality | Complies. In week 25 the Supplier verifies automated inspection, defect detection, OCR, barcode and image quality verification. Every card is verified in line, not sampled: engraved text by OCR, machine-readable codes on both faces, and the QR code compared byte for byte with its signed payload. Failing cards are rejected automatically and reproduced within the job. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 3.6 and 6.8; Attachment: Laser personalization system datasheet |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 13** Backup and recovery tests: backup, database and configuration restore, disaster recovery procedures | Complies. In week 20 the Supplier tests backup execution, database and configuration restoration, and the disaster recovery procedures, including failover of the database and service virtual machines to the secondary environment in Blantyre. The recovery point and recovery time achieved are measured by inducing failure, not checked by inspection. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 4.4 and 4.5; System Architecture Sections 9.3 and 12.3 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 14** End-to-end workflow simulation from NRIS extraction to dispatch and status update | Complies. In week 25 the Supplier simulates record retrieval from NRIS, job generation, personalization, quality inspection, mailing preparation, dispatch generation and status update to NRIS, against the NRIS test environment, reported in the Workflow Simulation Report. Status events are raised automatically at each stage, with no operator reporting. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 4.2 and 4.5; System Architecture Sections 2.2 and 7.11 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 15** Pilot production of 500 to 1,000 test cards through actual production processes | Complies. In week 26, 500 to 1,000 test cards are produced from NRIS test environment records through the full process to dispatch record and status update, operated by trainee operators, verifying data accuracy, print and engraving quality, mailing and reporting. As clarified in the Responses to Queries 003, test cards are not issued; they are destroyed under dual custody. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.6 and 4.5; System Architecture Section 6.10 |
| **4.2.2 Table A, Test 16** Performance tests: sustained and peak card rates, batch processing and mailing throughput | Complies. In week 26 the Supplier demonstrates sustained and peak production rates, batch processing and mailing throughput: not less than 1,000 cards per hour per personalization line, 2,000 combined, and 2,000 completed mailers per hour across the two mailing lines, over the fourteen-hour day. As clarified in the Responses to Queries 003, throughput counts accepted finished cards. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.5; System Architecture Sections 3.5, 11.1 and 11.6 |

## 4.3 Operational acceptance tests

| Requirement | Technical reasons supporting compliance | Cross-references |
|---|---|---|
| **4.3.0** Purchaser, assisted by Supplier, performs the listed tests after Installation for Operational Acceptance | Complies. Once the Installation Certificate is issued, the Purchaser performs the seventeen Table B tests in weeks 27 to 30 with the Supplier's assistance, and their successful completion is the condition for the Operational Acceptance Certificate. Each test is rehearsed in pre-commissioning, so acceptance confirms a known result rather than finding defects. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.8, 4.6 and 4.7 |
| **4.3.1** Live-production OAT confirming stability, throughput, security, integrity, NRIS interoperability and independence; prerequisite for acceptance and warranty | Complies. Inkript assists the Purchaser in running the seventeen Table B tests in weeks 27 to 30, against the NRIS test environment as the Responses to Queries 003 require, so that no card produced in testing is issued to a citizen. Each is measured by a method agreed with NRB in the operational acceptance test procedures issued in week 24. Their completion is the Operational Acceptance gate in week 30, from which post-Operational Acceptance technical assistance starts. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.6, 2.10, 4.6, 4.7 and 5.1 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 1** End-to-end workflow test: 100% records processed automatically with NRIS status and audit trail | Complies. In week 27 all test records flow from NRIS through personalization, quality control, mailing and dispatch to status update without manual intervention. Status events are published to NRIS automatically at each stage, and every stage writes against the National Identity Number and card serial number, giving a full audit trail per transaction. Rehearsed as pre-commissioning test 14. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 7.11 and 7.14 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 2** Sustained production stability: continuous operation, availability at least 99%, no severity 1 failures | Complies. In weeks 27 and 28 production runs continuously over the defined period, requiring availability of not less than 99 percent, measured with the exclusions in System Architecture Section 9.3, and no severity 1 failure. Clustered hosts, independent laser stations and two independent lines mean a component failure reduces throughput rather than stopping production. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 3.4 and 9.3 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 3** Throughput compliance: contractual cards per hour and mail items per hour, no backlog | Complies. In week 27 cards per hour and mail items per hour are measured across both lines over the fourteen-hour day against the contractual minimum of 2,000 per hour combined, counting accepted finished cards as clarified in the Responses to Queries 003. Mailing capacity matches personalization line for line, and buffer modules absorb short stoppages, so no backlog accumulates. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 3.5, 5.6 and 11.1 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 4** Peak load test: no failures or data loss, response times met, throughput at least 95% | Complies. In week 27 a simulated peak workload is applied with all components operating, requiring no failure or data loss, response times within the stated thresholds, and throughput of at least 95 percent of the nominal capacity of 2,000 cards per hour. Load, stress and transaction integrity testing at peak precede it. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 3.5, 11.2 and 11.6 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 5** Data accuracy test: at least 99.99% accuracy, no mismatch, loss or duplication | Complies. In week 27 data on produced cards, read by in-line verification, is compared with the NRIS source record, requiring at least 99.99 percent accuracy and no mismatch, loss or duplication. Every card is checked: engraved text by OCR against the source record, and the QR code carrying the biometric data byte for byte against its signed payload. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 4.5 and 6.8 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 6** NRIS business process test: new issuance, reprint, replacement, lost and damaged cards | Complies. In week 27 new issuance, reprint, replacement, and lost and damaged card transactions run in production mode, with real-time status in NRIS and no failed or orphaned transactions. Each transaction type is distinguished on the NRIS record, and every exception follows a defined handling path and is recorded rather than discarded. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 7.10, 7.11 and 7.12 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 7** Card quality and security: approved design, security features applied, reject rate at most 1%, barcode/QR functional | Complies. In week 27 cards are checked for compliance with the approved design, correctly applied laser engraving, micro text, UV and other features, and a reject rate of at most 1 percent of attempts. As amended by Addendum 2, machine-readable features are barcode or QR, with no chip; the QR code and card identifier are read on every card. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 3.6, 4.4 and 6.8 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 8** Mailing and dispatch test: at least 99.5% accuracy, correct card-envelope-recipient matching, dispatch records | Complies. In week 27 the test requires at least 99.5 percent accuracy in insertion and addressing, no duplicate or missing items, and correct card-to-envelope-to-recipient matching. Every card is read twice against its record, the second time on its carrier after affixing, and a dispatch record is generated and stored for every mail piece. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 5.4 and 5.7 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 9** Audit trail test: full card traceability, all user actions and events logged, tamper-proof logs | Complies. In week 27 the test demonstrates traceability of every card from NRIS record to dispatch, and logging of all operator, supervisor and administrator actions, system events and exceptions. Audit records are written once to storage that no user, administrators included, can modify or delete, with verifiable integrity and ten-year retention. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 7.14 and 10.6 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 10** Security operations test: authorized access only, failed logins alerted, no escalation or critical vulnerabilities | Complies. In week 27 the test confirms access limited to authorized roles, failed logins logged and alerted, no privilege escalation, all security events monitored, and no critical vulnerability. Independent penetration testing in week 24, with retest in weeks 25 and 26, precedes it, and privileged access is issued just in time and recorded. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 4.4 and 4.6; System Architecture Sections 10.3 and 10.7 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 11** Backup, restore and recovery during live operation without corruption or disruption | Complies. In week 27 a backup is executed during live operation and data is restored without corruption or significant disruption. Backups are scheduled, encrypted, integrity-checked and replicated to immutable storage at the secondary environment, and restoration supports file, database, full system and point-in-time recovery with verification before service resumes. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Section 12.3 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 12** Monitoring and alerting test: real-time alerts, dashboards, logs and metrics for troubleshooting | Complies. In week 27 the test confirms that alerts for failures, performance degradation and consumable thresholds reach operators in real time. Monitoring runs 24/7 on centralized dashboards, alerts are delivered on consoles and by email and SMS to designated NRB staff, and logs are aggregated centrally for troubleshooting. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 6.11 and 12.2 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 13** Multi-shift test: two shifts, smooth handover, consistent throughput, no vendor dependency | Complies. In weeks 27 and 28 two NRB operator shifts run production with handover procedures, requiring no disruption at handover, consistent throughput and no dependency on the Supplier. Beforehand every operator is assessed competent on the deployed system, shift handover included, and coached in production until two shifts run without Supplier intervention. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 3.1, 3.7, 3.8 and 4.6 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 14** Consumables and inventory test: usage tracking, low-stock alerts, real-time inventory reports | Complies. In week 27 the test verifies accurate consumables tracking, automated low-stock alerts, real-time inventory reports and no stoppage for want of stock. Stock Control holds every blank by serial number, tracks carriers, envelopes and other consumables against usage, and alerts against defined thresholds in time to reorder. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Section 6.10 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 15** Reporting and SLA test: daily, weekly, monthly reports, correct SLA metrics, no log discrepancies | Complies. In week 27 the test verifies daily, weekly and monthly reports, correct calculation of uptime, throughput and rejection rate, on-demand access for authorized users, and no discrepancy with the logs. Reports draw on the same production records that drive status feedback to NRIS, and are reconcilable against the system logs they derive from. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 4.6; System Architecture Sections 6.11 and 7.13 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 16** Operational independence test: Purchaser staff operate the full system, vendor observing only | Complies. In week 28 NRB staff operate the full system, perform every routine task and handle minor faults with the Supplier observing only, and the operational manuals are validated. Training is built backwards from this test: every trainee is assessed competent on the deployed system, and NRB trainers are certified. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 3.1, 3.7 and 4.6 |
| **4.3.2 Table B, Test 17** Final acceptance demonstration: continuous production over observation period, all KPIs met | Complies. In weeks 29 and 30 production runs continuously over a ten working day observation period, agreed at design approval, requiring availability of at least 99 percent, data accuracy of at least 99.99 percent, mailing accuracy of at least 99.5 percent and a reject rate of at most 1 percent. Formal acceptance is recorded at the week 30 gate. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 2.2, 2.7, 4.6 and 4.7 |
