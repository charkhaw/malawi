# 5. Service Specifications, Recurrent Cost Items

## 5.1 Warranty defect repair

| Requirement | Technical reasons supporting compliance | Cross-references |
|---|---|---|
| **5.1.1.1** Three-year warranty defect repair with response and resolution standards and service modes | Complies. Three years of warranty defect repair cover all equipment, systems, software and services supplied, repairing or replacing goods and correcting services at no cost, on the severity targets of Annex A to the Responses to Queries 003. Equipment and infrastructure are restored on site from spares, failed modules return to their manufacturer, and software is corrected on call. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 5.1 and 5.4; System Architecture Section 12.4; Attachment: Manufacturer's Authorization |

## 5.2 Technical support

| Requirement | Technical reasons supporting compliance | Cross-references |
|---|---|---|
| **5.2.1.1** User support and hotline available 24x7 | Complies. A user support hotline operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week, through the warranty and post-warranty periods. Every incident, by telephone, email or service desk, is logged with a reference, severity and owner, and response time runs from logging: 30 minutes for Severity 1 and one hour for Severity 2, at any hour. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 5.1 and 5.2; System Architecture Section 12.4 |
| **5.2.1.2** Level 2 and Level 3 technical assistance with OEE, MTTR, MTBF and PMC KPIs | Complies. Level 2 is the technical team in Lilongwe; Level 3 is the equipment manufacturer's product line specialists and the Supplier's senior engineers. Targets follow Annex A to the Responses to Queries 003: OEE 85 percent (monthly minimum 80), MTTR 4 hours for critical faults, MTBF at least 90 percent of manufacturer figures, PMC 95 percent monthly. | System Architecture Section 12.4; Preliminary Project Plan Sections 5.2 and 5.3 |
| **5.2.1.3** Three-year renewable post-warranty maintenance with response, resolution standards and service modes | Complies. Post-warranty maintenance runs for three years after the warranty period, renewable, on the same response and resolution standards and modes of service: on-site module replacement from site spares, return of failed modules to the manufacturer for repair, and on-call remote or on-site software correction. Availability of at least 99 percent applies throughout, reported monthly. | Preliminary Project Plan Sections 5.1 and 5.6; System Architecture Section 12.4 |

## 5.3 Requirements of the Supplier's technical team

| Requirement | Technical reasons supporting compliance | Cross-references |
|---|---|---|
| **5.3.1** Technical team for post-acceptance assistance, including legislative modifications, at inventory-table quantities | Complies. A technical team of the five specified roles, each meeting its role's qualifications, covers post-Operational Acceptance technical assistance from Lilongwe, giving two-hour on-site attendance for critical incidents. Modifications, such as those for changes in legislation or regulation, go through the Contract's change procedure and are then delivered under change control. | System Architecture Section 12.4; Preliminary Project Plan Section 5.2; Change Management and Configuration Governance Plan Sections 2 and 6 |
| **5.3.1.1** CPF Infrastructure Engineer: relevant degree; 5 years data center, integration, high availability; CCNA/CCNP, OEM certifications | Complies. The Card Production Facility Infrastructure Engineer holds a degree in computer engineering, IT or electrical engineering, Cisco CCNA or CCNP and relevant manufacturer certifications, with at least five years in data center and high availability systems. The role leads the hardened platform, secondary environment, backup and recovery, and trains NRB staff on the clusters, storage array and database. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 3.8; System Architecture Section 12.4; Cybersecurity Risk Management Plan Section 9.3; Attachment: Candidate Summary and CV |
| **5.3.1.2** ID Card Security Specialist: degree or diploma, ACE-M and PKI certifications, 5 years polycarbonate card security | Complies. The ID Card Security Specialist holds a degree or diploma in computer science or information security, ACE-M and PKI certifications, and at least five years implementing multilayer polycarbonate card security. The specialist also trains NRB staff on quality assurance and the inspection of card security features, such as the UV, deliberate error, tactile laser and signed QR elements. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 3.8; System Architecture Sections 4.2, 4.4 and 12.4; Attachment: Candidate Summary and CV |
| **5.3.1.3** Facilities/Electrical Engineer: engineering degree, 7 years critical infrastructure, UPS, HVAC, generators, fire suppression | Complies. The post-Operational Acceptance technical team includes a Facilities and Electrical Engineer meeting the qualification and certification requirements set for the role. The facility systems the role supports are those the Information System relies on: UPS, generator with automatic transfer, HVAC and precision cooling, clean agent fire suppression and physical security installations. | System Architecture Sections 9.5 and 12.4; Attachment: Candidate Summary and CV |
| **5.3.1.4** Network Engineer: relevant degree or diploma, CCNA and CCNP, 5 years VLANs, firewalls, redundancy | Complies. The Network Engineer holds a degree or diploma in computer science, network engineering or telecommunications, CCNA and CCNP, and at least five years on VLANs, firewalls and secure government networks. The role covers the zone VLANs, firewall policy and wide area links, including the redundant core switch and firewall pairs. | Preliminary Project Plan Section 3.8; Cybersecurity Risk Management Plan Section 9.3; System Architecture Section 9.2; Attachment: Candidate Summary and CV |
| **5.3.1.5** Cybersecurity and PKI Specialist: relevant degree, 7 years, CISSP, CISM, CEH, 3 years identity or PKI | Complies. The Cybersecurity and PKI Specialist holds a degree in cybersecurity, computer science or information security, CISSP, CISM and CEH, and at least seven years in cybersecurity including three in identity management or PKI, with experience of hardware security modules, encryption and digital certificates. The role provides Level 2 security support through the warranty period. | Cybersecurity Risk Management Plan Section 9.3; Preliminary Project Plan Section 3.8; System Architecture Section 12.4; Attachment: Candidate Summary and CV |
