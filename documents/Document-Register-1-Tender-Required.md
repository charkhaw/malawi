# Document Register 1 — Required by the Tender

RFP No. MW-PPPC-546386-GO-RFB · Purchaser: Public Private Partnership Commission

## Classification

| Tag | Meaning | Consequence if missing |
|---|---|---|
| **[M] Mandatory** | The tender requires it. | Non-responsiveness, or rejection of the subcontractor. **Uncurable** — ITP 30.3: a non-responsive Technical Part "may not subsequently be made responsive by correction of the material deviation, reservation, or omission". |
| **[S] Scored** | Named as evidence under a scored sub-criterion, with its weight. | Not fatal, but loses points directly. The technical threshold is 70%. |

## Two rules that govern every item below

> **Certificates must be physically included.** Section IV p.110: *"Whenever a technical requirement
> is for the Supplier to provide certifications (e.g., ISO 9001), copies of these certifications must
> be included in the Technical Proposal."* A certificate number is not compliance.

> **No pricing anywhere in the Technical Part.** ITP 11.4 — material price information in the
> Technical Part makes the Proposal non-responsive.

## The three items with the shortest fuse

All depend on third parties and cannot be compressed by working harder:

1. **Card OEM laboratory reports (2.13)** — 14 tests, from a recognised laboratory, dated within
   three years, at the supplier's own cost. If any are missing or stale, re-testing may take longer
   than the programme allows.
2. **Card OEM national ID references (2.11)** — actual letters or purchase orders from other
   governments. These take time to obtain and are not in the supplier's gift alone.
3. **Manufacturer's Authorisation (1.1)** — an original on OEM letterhead with a binding signature.
   Routine, but it moves at the OEM legal department's pace, not ours.

## One gap this register cannot close

Several mandatory items depend on answers we do not yet have from PPPC — in particular whether the
ICT infrastructure, HSMs and a disaster recovery site fall within our scope of supply. If they do,
this register is missing an entire hardware and software category, and no supplier has been
approached for it. Tracked in the clarification questions, not here.
