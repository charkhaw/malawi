Matica´s card mailing systems are a simple and affordable solution for direct card mailing and fulfillment applications. These systems are designed to work with mid to high volume card issuance machines such as Matica's S6000 and S7000 Series. They are often used in telecommunication, retail, healthcare and insurance institutions, and government services. The MS1000 model is ideal for small volume delivery of pre-personalized financial cards. This entry-level system offers a centralized card issuance solution. This system is a simple and affordable solution for stand-alone direct card mailing and fulfillment applications. It carries all necessary steps for card delivery from production to mailing, which includes card reading, transport, paper folding and envelope insertion.  

This system is ideal for the following applications:

National ID
Transit passes
Health care cards
Loyalty cards
Driver's license
Discount passes
Gift cards
Vehicle registration cards
Insurance cards
 

Features:
Preconfigured, dedicated PC using MaticardPro software
Entry-level system (MS1000 model)
All-in-one reading, attaching, printing, folding, inserting system
