The S7000 is the ultimate central issuance systems and can be configured to perfectly match your current needs. By supporting constant upgrades, this machine enables you to add functionalities to your system to improve its performance. Its continuous development and its ability to integrate all of the latest technologies make it ideal for your current needs and those you may require in the future. This extremely versatile system offers an unlimited number of configurations and more than 50 different options. For example, this machine can be used with a wide range of card types from flat to embossed cards. It is the complete solution for card personalization and fulfillment applications.

This card system is ideal for the issuance of driver's licenses, national identity cards, bank and payment cards, health cards, loyalty cards, prepaid cards, and much more. Watch the promotional video for an overview of how this printer works and a summary of its features.  

 
Features:
Printing method: embossing, tipping, indenting, laser engraving, thermal transfer
Resolution: 300 dpi
Printing speed: up tp 2000 cards per hour
Encoding: magnetic cards, smart contact / contactless cards, single or multi
Card format: Plastic CR80 or metal cards
Card feeder & card stacker capacity: 600 cards
Communication interface: Ethernet
Software: diagnostic and setup software included, MaticardPro, laser engraving software, smart card SDK (optional)
Single software fully compliant with Visa/Mastercard requirements
Flexible configuration and on-site upgrade
Consumables: SmartSupply
