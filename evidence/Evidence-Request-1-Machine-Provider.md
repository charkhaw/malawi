## Why the detail matters

This tender scores the technical proposal out of 100 and weights it at 70% of the award decision.
**Thirty of those hundred points are awarded against your machine's capabilities**, assessed on the
documentary evidence we submit. The tender also states, in terms:

> "A response of 'yes' or 'will do' is unlikely to convey the credibility of the response."

> "Whenever a technical requirement is for the Supplier to provide certifications, copies of these
> certifications must be included in the Technical Proposal."

So please send **documents**, not assurances — datasheets, certificates, test records, drawings.
Where a capability is described, we need the datasheet page we can cite it from. Everything must be
in English.

One further point that governs how we will use your material: our clause-by-clause compliance
statement **legally prevails over any datasheet** in the event of a conflict. We will therefore
write only what your documents support. If a requirement below is one you do not meet, tell us
plainly — we will handle it in the proposal. An overstatement discovered at Factory Acceptance
Testing is far more damaging than a qualified answer now.

## Section 1 — Scored capability evidence (highest priority)

These four items carry the 30 points. For each, we need the specific supporting evidence, ideally
with a datasheet page or test report reference.

**1.1 Laser personalization feature support.** Confirmation and evidence that the machine produces,
on 100% polycarbonate cards:
- Laser engraved text
- Greyscale photograph
- Stacked-layer security features including, at minimum: secondary image, micro lettering, CLI
  (Changing Laser Image), QR code carrying both alphanumeric values, and tactile laser printing
- Multilingual identity documents

**1.2 Marking resolution.** Documented evidence of:
- Photograph: **600 dpi or higher, in greyscale** (Addendum No. 2 amended §3.2.2.1.1 from full color to greyscale)
- Micro text: **600 dpi or higher**, at **0.33 mm line width and height**

Sample output or a measurement report would be materially stronger than a specification line alone.

**1.3 Independent card processing capability.** Evidence that:
- Each integrated laser personalizes a complete card independently, without reliance on other lasers
- Any single laser station can be switched on, switched off, or taken out of service for maintenance
  **without interrupting or degrading overall production flow**

**1.4 Duplex printing speed.** Evidence of **not less than 2,000 cards per hour, duplex, across two
machines** — and, separately, **not less than 1,000 cards per hour per personalization line**.
Please state the basis of the figure (nominal, sustained, or measured) and the card layout it assumes.

## Section 3 — Further personalization system requirements

Confirmation and supporting evidence for each:

- Modular machine design permitting future expansion and upgrades
- Fiber-optic or diode-based laser with greyscale printing technology
- In-line data verification of each personalized card, including verification of optical data
- Automatic card recognition to identify the blank card body and check it against the card reference;
  independent X-Y alignment for card front and card back, and separately for the CLI feature
- Automatic rejection of defective cards with automatic or manual reproduction within the same
  production job, notified to the operator by on-screen message
- GUI-based machine software allowing staff to retrieve the status of each record for database
  update, generating audit or XML/flat files on completion of each job or batch
- Emergency stop buttons positioned around the machine, operable from every point
- Graphical Layout Designer/Editor allowing configuration of text fields, images, CLI windows,
  micro-text positions, MRZ zones, QR zones and all other variable data elements
- Integrated cooling system suitable for continuous industrial-scale laser engraving
- Laser calibration software able to adjust individual laser units or all units simultaneously,
  automated and operator-assisted, generating logs for audit purposes
- Batch processing of 500 to 1,000 cards per batch
- Sustained throughput of 28,000 cards per day on two-shift operation
- Continuous 24/7 capability during peak periods with no degradation over 8–12 hour production cycles

## Section 4 — Automated mailing and dispatch system (2 units)

Datasheet plus confirmation of each capability:

- Card affixing to carrier letters, 1 to 4 cards per letter
- Automatic printing of personalized carrier letters
- Automatic conveyance of documents through the inserting module for envelope insertion
- Envelope sealing; address printing; printing of courier labels and tracking references
- Fully integrated card transport modules
- Verification capabilities including 1D, 2D and QR code verification
- High-speed matching of card-to-person mail items
- Continuous sorting and dispatch sorting
- Reject magazines for error handling; buffer modules for uninterrupted operation
- Batch processing; compatibility with modern courier workflows
- Job data received from an external system over a live interface, without manual re-keying

**Throughput evidence:** at least **2,000 completed mailers per hour with two machines**, and
**28,000 envelopes per day minimum with two machines on two shifts**, with no accumulation of
unprocessed cards or mail pieces in continuous operation.

**Acceptance-test capability:** the tender sets ≥99.5% accuracy in mail insertion and addressing, no
duplicate or missing mail items, correct card-to-envelope-to-recipient matching, and a dispatch
record generated per mail piece and available for export. Please confirm these are demonstrable.
