---

## Why this pack is critical

The tender names the supply of polycarbonate cards as a **major item requiring a nominated
subcontractor who must independently satisfy stated minimum criteria**. Its wording is unambiguous:

> "Failure to comply with these requirements will result in the rejection of the subcontractor."

Because the cards are indispensable to the scope, a rejected card supplier is effectively a lost bid.
Section 1 below is therefore not a wish list — every line is a qualification gate, and each needs a
document rather than a statement. The tender also requires that **copies of certifications be
physically included in the proposal**, so scanned certificates are needed, not certificate numbers.

Quantity: **two million chipless cards**. The Purchaser has confirmed this procurement is for chipless polycarbonate cards, and that all requirements relating to integrated circuits are excluded from scope.

---

## Section 1 — Qualification criteria (each one is a pass/fail gate)

**1.1** Written confirmation that you are the **OEM of the offered polycarbonate cards**.

**1.2** Valid **ISO 27001**, **ISO 9001** and **ISO 14001** certificates — scanned copies of all
three, showing validity dates covering the proposal period.

**1.3** Confirmation of a **minimum 10-year card lifespan** evidenced under **ISO 24789**.

**1.4** Confirmation you can produce the **OVMI (Optically Variable Magnetic Ink) Malawi flag motif**.

**1.5** Confirmation of **stacked-layer greyscale personalization** capability (Addendum No. 2 amended this criterion from full color to greyscale).

**1.6** Confirmation of **high-resolution portrait capability at 600+ dpi**.

**1.7** Evidence that you **own at least one manufacturing site** capable of producing polycarbonate
cards with advanced security features — site name, location, and supporting evidence.

**1.8** Evidence of **card production capacity within your own manufacturing sites**. The tender
wording is *"a card production capacity of up to fifty (50) million cards per year"*. Please state
your actual annual capacity and provide supporting evidence.

**1.9** **At least two (2) National Identity references** for the supply of polycarbonate cards,
totalling **at least five (5) million cards collectively**. The tender requires **letters of
reference or purchase orders** — please supply the actual documents. For each reference: the issuing
authority, country, contract period and card volume.

**1.10** A **letterhead undertaking** confirming you will provide **5,000 sample/specimen cards free
of charge**, to the approved design, for laser personalization machine testing.

## Section 2 — Card construction and standards

**2.1** Confirmation of construction: **100% fused polycarbonate, multi-layer, six-layer, fully fused
and manufactured without adhesives** to prevent delamination.

**2.2** Compliance with **ISO 7810**, **ISO 10373-1** and **ISO 24789-1 & 2**.

**2.3 Laboratory test reports.** This is the largest single documentary requirement. The tender
requires test results from *"one of the leading Polycarbonate Card Labs of the world"*, **covering
the last three years**, to be provided **at the time of bid submission**, for all fourteen tests:

| # | Test |
|---|---|
| i | Card Usage & Aging Simulation (minimum 10-year service life) |
| ii | Card Dimensions |
| iii | Card Bending Stiffness |
| iv | Card Resistance to Chemicals (except salt mist) |
| v | Card Resistance to Chemicals (salt mist) |
| vi | Card Dimensional Stability & Warpage (temperature & humidity) |
| vii | Card Ultraviolet (UV) Light Exposure |
| viii | Card Dynamic Bending Stress (up to 1,000 cycles) |
| ix | Card Dynamic Torsional Stress (up to 1,000 cycles) |
| x | Card Peel Strength |
| xi | Card Adhesion (Blocking) |
| xii | Card Opacity |
| xiii | Card Warpage |
| xiv | Card Resistance to Heat |

Please confirm immediately if any of these fourteen is missing from your current test file, or if any
report is older than three years. Testing is at the supplier's own cost under the tender, and lead
time on a re-test could be decisive.

## Section 3 — Card body security features

Confirmation of capability for each. **Front:**

- Guilloche background; rainbow (IRIS) printing
- Optically Variable Magnetic Ink (OVMI) with Malawi flag motif
- CLI (Changing Laser Image) — photo and number
- Microtext; relief tactile text; deliberate error elements
- Invisible UV elements (national emblem)
- Numismatic background pattern; crystal / photonic crystal pattern elements
- Thermochromic ink
- Micro-text within the portrait box; tactile micro-text in relief

**Back:**

- Rainbow color transition effect
- OVMI (Malawi map or rising red half-sun symbol)
- Invisible UV printing (Malawi map or rising red half-sun symbol)
- Photonic crystal patterns and Guilloche security structures, micro-text, deliberate error elements

**Personalization security features** (applied on our laser line, but the card body must support them):
high-resolution portrait 600+ dpi · stacked-layer greyscale personalization · secondary image · CLI
elements · micro-lettering above 600 dpi · QR code and multilingual data · tactile laser printing.
