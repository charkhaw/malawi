# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Scope of supply

The Information System supplied, installed, integrated and commissioned for the Card Production
Facility at the National Registration Bureau, Lilongwe, and for its secondary environment at the
Purchaser's disaster recovery data center, comprises:

| Scope | Supplied |
|---|---|
| **Equipment** | Two automatic modular laser personalization systems; two automated mailing and dispatch systems |
| **Materials** | Two million chipless polycarbonate card bodies, serialized at manufacture |
| **Software** | Card Personalization Management System; Card Layout Template Editor; Data Preparation Service; Signing Service; Printer Control Service; Laser Personalization Control Software; Quality Control Management; Mailing and Dispatch Management System; Stock Control and Card Accountability; Monitoring, Reporting and Analytics; Administration and Access Control; Presentation Layer |
| **Infrastructure** | Compute, storage, database, network and security infrastructure at the primary site and the secondary environment, including the hardware security modules, the non-production environments and the Security Operations Center |
| **Integration** | Interfaces to the National Registration and Identification System; dispatch and delivery tracking, with readiness for courier or postal service interfaces; the Government public key infrastructure, for the digital signature on each card; NRB's Security Operations Center, to which security alerts and events are forwarded |

## 1.2 Integration and interoperability responsibility

Pursuant to ITP 16.2(b), we confirm that, if awarded the Contract, we accept responsibility for the
successful integration and interoperability of all components and proposed Information Technologies
of the Information System, as required by the request for proposals document and as further
specified in the Technical Requirements.

This responsibility covers:

- the personalization and mailing equipment, the interface between them, and their interfaces to the application software;
- the polycarbonate cards and their compatibility with the personalization equipment supplied;
- all application software, whether developed, configured or supplied under license;
- the compute, storage, database, network and security infrastructure, at the primary site and in the secondary environment;
- the hardware security modules and the interface to the Government public key infrastructure;
- the interfaces through which environmental, power and physical security events from the facility works reach the system's monitoring;
- the interfaces to the National Registration and Identification System;
- the interfaces to dispatch and delivery tracking services; and
- the forwarding of security alerts and events to NRB's Security Operations Center.

Responsibility is accepted as a single point of accountability to the Purchaser, irrespective of the
number of manufacturers, subcontractors or joint venture members supplying individual components.
